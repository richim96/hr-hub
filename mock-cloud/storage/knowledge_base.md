# People Team Knowledge Base

Use this information to help answer employee questions.

---

## Time Off & Leave

- **Annual leave:** 30 days for full-time employees
- **Sick leave:** Notify manager by 9 AM; doctor's note required after 3 days
- **Special leave:**
  - Marriage: 2 days
  - Birth of child (partner): 10 days
  - Bereavement (immediate family): 3 days
  - Moving house: 1 day

---

## Payroll

- Salaries paid on the **25th of each month**
- Expense reimbursement: Submit within 30 days, processed in 5-7 business days
- Salary reviews happen in **January**

---

## Documents & Letters

| Document | Processing Time |
|----------|-----------------|
| Employment confirmation letter | 2-3 business days |
| Salary certificate | 2-3 business days |
| Reference letter | 5-7 business days |

Request via people@company.com or the People team ticketing system.

---

## Address Changes

- Update your address in HRIS or contact People team
- **Important:** Address changes may affect your tax bracket (Steuerklasse)
- Update within 2 weeks of moving
- We will update: payroll, emergency contacts, mail delivery

---

## Tax Information (Germany)

| Tax Class | Who |
|-----------|-----|
| Class 1 | Single, divorced, widowed |
| Class 3/5 | Married (one higher income, one lower) |
| Class 4 | Married (similar incomes) |

Address changes may require tax class review. Contact payroll@company.com for tax-related questions.

## Remote Work

- Hybrid: Minimum 2 days in office per week
- Full remote requires director approval
- Working abroad: Up to 2 weeks with manager approval; longer needs legal review
